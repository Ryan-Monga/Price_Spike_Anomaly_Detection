# Price Spike Anomaly Detection for Pump-and-Dump Schemes

A comprehensive, production-grade financial surveillance and machine learning system designed to detect anomalous market movements, volume surges, and artificial pump-and-dump manipulation patterns in equity and cryptocurrency assets.

---

## 1. Project Overview

Pump-and-dump schemes are fraudulent market manipulations where bad actors artificially inflate the price of an asset through coordinated aggressive buying and promotional hype ("pump"), followed by rapid dumping of their holdings onto unsuspecting investors ("dump"), triggering a sharp price collapse.

This application provides an end-to-end, multi-tier anomaly detection pipeline combining:
1. **Statistical Baseline Rules**: Rolling z-scores, volume surges, and price-volume divergence.
2. **One-Class Support Vector Machine (SVM)**: Unsupervised high-dimensional distribution boundary detection.
3. **Deep LSTM Autoencoder (PyTorch)**: Sequence-to-sequence temporal pattern reconstruction error analysis.
4. **Calibrated Hybrid Ensemble**: Weighted risk score combining statistical, distributional, and temporal signals.
5. **Controlled Synthetic Benchmark Lab**: 6 parameterized anomaly scenarios for empirical surveillance evaluation with ground-truth verification.
6. **Experimental Sequence GAN**: Generative Adversarial Network for baseline sequence generation and data augmentation research.

> **Surveillance Disclaimer**: The alerts generated by this system are model-derived risk indicators based on historical and technical deviations. They do not constitute legal or definitive regulatory proof of market manipulation without corroborating off-chain and forensic investigations.

## 2. Features and Model Descriptions

### Feature A: Market Data Acquisition (`src/data_fetcher.py`)
- Real-time and historical daily OHLCV retrieval via `yfinance`.
- Supported default assets: `AAPL`, `TSLA`, `MSFT`, `BTC-USD`, `ETH-USD`, plus arbitrary custom ticker entry.
- Historical horizons: 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, 1 year, 2 years, and 5 years.
- Local CSV caching in `data/raw/` to avoid redundant network requests.
- Offline fallback benchmark generator based on Geometric Brownian Motion and stochastic volume for offline testing.

### Feature B: Causal Feature Engineering (`src/features.py`)
- Computes 10 technical and statistical indicators strictly using backward-looking windows to prevent lookahead target leakage:
  1. `log_return`: $\ln(P_t / P_{t-1})$
  2. `simple_return`: $(P_t - P_{t-1}) / P_{t-1}$
  3. `volume_change`: $(V_t - V_{t-1}) / (V_{t-1} + \epsilon)$
  4. `volatility_30`: 30-day rolling return standard deviation
  5. `rolling_mean_30`: 30-day rolling return mean
  6. `rolling_std_30`: 30-day rolling return standard deviation
  7. `return_zscore`: Normalized return relative to recent volatility
  8. `relative_volume`: Ratio of current volume to 30-day rolling volume mean
  9. `abs_return`: Absolute return magnitude $|R_t|$
  10. `hl_range`: Intraday price span $(H_t - L_t) / C_t$
- Robust protection against zero division, zero prices, missing observations, and infinite values.

### Feature C: Statistical Baseline Detector (`src/baseline.py`)
- Rule-based surveillance flagging:
  - Abnormal positive/negative returns accompanied by relative volume surges.
  - Returns that are extreme relative to recent volatility ($|z| \ge 2.5$).
  - Abnormal volume churn ($V_{\text{rel}} \ge 2.0x$) despite stagnant price changes ($|R_t| < 1.5\%$).
- Returns continuous score in $[0, 1]$, binary flags, and natural language diagnostic explanations.

### Feature D: One-Class Support Vector Machine (`src/one_class_svm.py`)
- Fits a hypersphere boundary on presumed-normal historical training observations.
- Standardized via training-only fitted `RobustScaler`.
- Score convention: Negated decision function $-f(x)$ calibrated to $[0, 1]$ where higher values strictly indicate greater anomalousness.
- Model persistence via `joblib`.

### Feature E: PyTorch LSTM Autoencoder (`src/lstm_autoencoder.py`)
- Multi-layer recurrent encoder-decoder mapping causal sequence windows (default 30 observations) into a compressed latent bottleneck and reconstructing them.
- Uses reconstruction Mean Squared Error (MSE) as a temporal anomaly indicator: sequences that deviate from normal temporal dynamics suffer high reconstruction loss.
- Evaluates sequences strictly up to timestamp $t$ with score assigned to the final observation.
- Tracks epoch-by-epoch training and validation loss curves.
- Weights and metadata saved via `torch.save`.

### Feature F: Hybrid Ensemble Detector (`src/hybrid_detector.py`)
- Composite risk score:
  $$\text{hybrid\_score} = w_{\text{stat}} \cdot s_{\text{stat}} + w_{\text{svm}} \cdot s_{\text{svm}} + w_{\text{lstm}} \cdot s_{\text{lstm}}$$
  where $\sum w_i = 1.0$.
- Graceful dynamic fallback: automatically reweights available models if advanced models have not yet been trained.
- Automated threshold calibration on normal validation data (default 95th percentile).
- Generates evidence-based explanations detailing individual model contributions.

### Feature G: Experimental Sequence GAN (`src/gan_extension.py`)
- 1D sequence Generative Adversarial Network featuring a GRU-based Generator and Discriminator.
- Explores synthetic baseline sequence generation and training data augmentation.
- Evaluates surveillance metrics with and without GAN-augmented training manifolds.

### Feature H: Synthetic Anomaly Benchmark & Evaluation Lab (`src/anomaly_injection.py`, `src/evaluation.py`)
- Controlled injection of 6 distinct scenarios into chronological test splits:
  1. **Multi-Day Pump & Dump Reversal**: Progressive price climb with accelerating volume followed by sharp dump reversal.
  2. **Sudden Upward Shock**: Rapid spike with volume surge.
  3. **Sudden Downward Flash Dump**: Sharp panic selloff with extreme volume.
  4. **Heavy Volume Churn**: Massive volume expansion with flat price movement.
  5. **Extreme Return Shock (No Volume)**: Large price movement without volume backing.
  6. **Benign High Volatility**: Volatile market regime used to evaluate false positive resistance (ground-truth label = 0).
- Metrics computed: Precision, Recall, F1-Score, F4-Score (prioritizing recall for financial fraud detection), False Positive Rate (FPR), Confusion Matrix, and Average Detection Delay.

## 7. Operational Workflow: Step-by-Step Demonstration

### Step 1: Ingest & Inspect Market Data
1. In the left sidebar, select an asset (e.g. `AAPL`, `TSLA`, `BTC-USD`) or type a custom symbol.
2. Select a time horizon (default: `1y`).
3. Click **"📥 Load & Preprocess Data"**.
4. Navigate to **"📊 Overview"** to inspect latest price, return, relative volume, and the current detection status.
5. Switch to **"📈 Market Analysis"** to inspect historical candlestick/line prices, trading volume, volatility envelopes ($\pm 2.5\sigma$), and anomaly flags.

### Step 2: Investigate Flagged Events
1. Open the **"🔍 Anomaly Investigation"** tab.
2. Review the sortable anomaly summary table listing flagged dates, returns, volume multipliers, and model risk scores.
3. Select an anomaly date from the dropdown to view its $\pm 7$-day local context chart and evidence breakdown.

### Step 3: Train Machine Learning Models
1. Navigate to **"🧠 Model Training"**.
2. Notice the chronological split breakdown: **70% Training**, **15% Validation**, and **15% Test**.
3. Under **One-Class SVM**, adjust the contamination parameter ($\nu$) and click **"🚀 Train One-Class SVM"**.
4. Under **PyTorch LSTM Autoencoder**, configure the sequence length (e.g. 25 days) and epochs (e.g. 20), then click **"🚀 Train LSTM Autoencoder"**. Monitor real-time training and validation loss curves.
5. In the **Hybrid Tuning** section, adjust weights or click **"🎯 Calibrate on Validation"** to automatically set the threshold at the 95th percentile of normal validation observations.

### Step 4: Run Controlled Benchmark Experiments
1. Open the **"🧪 Evaluation Lab"** tab.
2. Choose one of the 6 controlled scenarios (e.g., `1. Multi-Day Pump & Dump Reversal`).
3. Set duration (e.g., 3 days) and severity strength (e.g., 1.5).
4. Click **"🔬 Run Benchmark Experiment"**.
5. Examine the comparative table evaluating Precision, Recall, F1, F4, False Positive Rate, and Confusion Matrix across the Baseline, SVM, LSTM, and Hybrid models.
6. (Optional) Click **"🚀 Run GAN Augmentation Experiment"** to test whether generative sequence augmentation enhances baseline manifold coverage.

### Step 5: Export Data and Reports
1. Switch to **"📁 Data & Reports"**.
2. Preview raw OHLCV and engineered causal feature matrices.
3. Download the full predictions CSV or the evaluation benchmark CSV.
4. Review past logged experiments stored persistently in the local SQLite database (`surveillance.db`).

## 8. Interpreting Surveillance Evaluation Metrics

In financial surveillance, false negatives (missed manipulation events) can be catastrophic, while false positives (benign spikes flagged for human review) create operational cost. Our evaluation module reports:

- **Precision**: $\frac{\text{TP}}{\text{TP} + \text{FP}}$ — Fraction of flagged alerts that were actual manipulation events.
- **Recall (Sensitivity)**: $\frac{\text{TP}}{\text{TP} + \text{FN}}$ — Fraction of actual manipulation events successfully caught by the system.
- **F1-Score**: Harmonic mean of Precision and Recall:
  $$\text{F1} = 2 \cdot \frac{\text{Precision} \cdot \text{Recall}}{\text{Precision} + \text{Recall}}$$
- **F4-Score**: Generalized $F_\beta$ metric with $\beta = 4$:
  $$F_4 = (1 + 4^2) \cdot \frac{\text{Precision} \cdot \text{Recall}}{4^2 \cdot \text{Precision} + \text{Recall}} = 17 \cdot \frac{\text{Precision} \cdot \text{Recall}}{16 \cdot \text{Precision} + \text{Recall}}$$
  $F_4$ weights **Recall four times more heavily than Precision**, prioritizing the detection of abusive trading schemes over false alarms.
- **False Positive Rate (FPR)**: $\frac{\text{FP}}{\text{FP} + \text{TN}}$ — Proportion of normal trading days mistakenly flagged.
- **Average Detection Delay**: Average number of trading days elapsed from the start of an abusive campaign before the detector raises an alert.

## 10. Known Limitations

1. **Daily Resolution**: The current implementation operates on daily closing bars. While effective for multi-day pump-and-dump accumulation and dumps, intraday microsecond wash-trading requires tick-level L2/L3 order book feeds.
2. **Unsupervised Boundary Contamination**: One-Class SVM assumes the training partition contains predominantly normal observations. Severe market crises or unlabelled historical manipulations within the training window can slightly contaminate the decision boundary.
3. **Synthetic vs. Real-World Manipulation**: High scores on synthetic scenarios demonstrate algorithm responsiveness to specific mathematical shocks, but real-world market manipulators dynamically adapt tactics to evade surveillance.
